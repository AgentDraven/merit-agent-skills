# .merit_launch.md

This is your private MERIT launch file. Keep it on your machine. Do not commit it.

How to use:

```powershell
..\merit-agent-skills\merit.ps1 apply --path .
..\merit-agent-skills\merit.ps1 deploy --path .
```

Linux/macOS:

```bash
../merit-agent-skills/merit.sh apply --path .
../merit-agent-skills/merit.sh deploy --path .
```

Lines beginning with `#` are comments. Edit the values after `=`.
